Frequently asked questions

What is the Progressive Pomodoro technique?

The Progressive Pomodoro technique is a variation of the classic Pomodoro method where session lengths grow as your focus improves. You start short, rate how well each session went, and your timer gradually builds toward longer, deeper work sessions.

How does the timer adapt to my focus?

After each session you rate your focus. Strong ratings, like Flow or Focused, increase your next session length. Lower ratings, like Distracted, keep the next session short. Over time the timer drifts toward the session length that matches your real focus.

Is Progressive Pomodoro free?

Yes. ProgPomo is completely free, open source under GPLv3, and has no accounts, ads, or sign-up. All your data stays in your browser's local storage.

Does it work offline?

Yes. ProgPomo is a progressive web app. Once loaded, it works offline and can be installed to your home screen on desktop and mobile.

What are binaural beats and do I need headphones?

Binaural beats play a slightly different frequency in each ear, and your brain perceives the difference as a beat. Headphones are required, because each ear must hear its own tone for the effect to work.

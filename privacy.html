Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026.

Short version

Your data stays on your device. ProgPomo has no accounts, no login, and no server storing your information. Your settings, tasks, and session history live only in your browser's local storage. I will never steal, sell, or share your data. Period.

What I collect

Right now: nothing. The app runs entirely in your browser. Nothing about your sessions, ratings, tasks, or settings is sent to any server.

Where your data lives

All of your data is stored in your own browser using local storage — the same place your browser keeps website preferences. It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it. ProgPomo works offline because everything it needs is on your device.

Analytics

I may add privacy-friendly analytics in the future to understand basic usage, like how many people visit the site and which pages they look at. If that happens, it will be limited to anonymous, aggregate statistics — no personal identifiers, and never your timer sessions, tasks, or ratings. If analytics are added, this page will be updated to say exactly what is collected.

The promise

I will never steal your data. I will never sell your data — not in the future, not to anyone, for any price. This app is free, has no ads, and no incentive to monetize you. Your focus data is yours.

Your data, your control

Because nothing is stored remotely, you already have full control. Use the Data tab in Settings to export or clear your data. Delete the site's data from your browser to remove everything.

Questions?

Contact me via GitHub or LinkedIn. See the about page for who I am.
